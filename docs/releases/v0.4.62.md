# Elesis v0.4.62

- Expands Elesis to a complete 88-card pool, including Basic, Common, Uncommon, Rare, and Ancient cards with their upgraded forms.
- Adds 18 distinct durable card Powers plus 11 supporting status Powers for Burn, Counter Attack, Retain, Exhaust, Block, and multi-enemy combat synergies.
- Adds 84 original card illustrations with distinct Attack, Skill, and Power art direction, plus matching runtime portraits.
- Updates English card, Power, and keyword localization for the complete pool.
- Raises the minimum BaseLib version to 3.3.8 and documents the finalized gameplay and card-art conventions.
