# Terminology

- `Vitality`: Elesis card style that builds Chivalry and can spend 5 Chivalry to draw a card.
- `Destruction`: Elesis card style that spends 5 Chivalry for burst damage, and sometimes Block.
- `Flame`: Elesis counter built by Flame cards and consumed by non-Flame attacks for extra damage.
- `Parry`: defensive knight technique label for Block-focused cards that build Chivalry.
- `Chivalry`: Elesis stance rhythm counter used by Vitality and Destruction cards.
